# Источники визуалов января

- `january-winter-night-original.png` — сгенерированный для проекта декоративный фон. Публичная оптимизированная версия: `public/assets/generated/january-winter-night.webp`.
- `snow-rolls-original.png` — изображение заснеженного Rolls-Royce, предоставленное заказчиком. Публичная оптимизированная версия: `public/assets/visuals/snow-rolls.webp`.

Генерация фона: встроенный ImageGen, без загруженных референсов. Промпт:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative photographic background for the January chapter of a responsive editorial website, wide panoramic 16:9 composition. Primary request: create a richly atmospheric winter and New Year backdrop, not an event photograph. Scene: deep midnight-blue snowy forest, distant frost-covered fir trees and low sculpted snowdrifts along the far left and right edges, faint warm golden festive lights deep in the distance and subtle icy haze. Composition: leave the broad central 65% dark, visually calm, and low contrast so large white website typography and a separate foreground photo remain readable; give the side edges dimensional snow and fine frost detail; no strong subject in the middle. Lighting: cold blue moonlight mixed with restrained warm gold glints, cinematic and believable, high-end stage photography, natural granular snow texture, refined depth, no cartoon shapes. Constraints: no people, no cars, no logos, no text, no numbers, no awards, no fake documentation of an event. Avoid bright white center, oversized snowflakes, dense visual noise, and synthetic-looking smooth snow panels.

Этот фон и фотография автомобиля — декоративные иллюстрации, не архивные кадры стрима.
